# Milonga email — what we've learned

**The agent's long-term memory for email.** Not metrics — metrics live in the monthly reports. This
is the *conclusions*: what works on this list, what doesn't, and what we're still testing.

**Read this before planning a month, writing subject lines, or writing copy.** Write to it when a
report proves something, or when Bernat says something worth keeping.

- **Proven** and **Disproven** are the two lists that change decisions. Keep them short — a rule
  nobody can hold in their head isn't memory, it's an archive
- Every entry carries **evidence and a date**, so a future session can tell a proven pattern from a
  hunch someone typed once
- **Open bets** is what we're testing now. It moves to Proven or Disproven when a month settles it
- When new evidence contradicts an entry, **replace it and quote the old text in the log.** Never
  leave both — a memory with contradictions makes the next session guess
