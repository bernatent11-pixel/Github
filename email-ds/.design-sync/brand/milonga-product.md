# Milonga — product & positioning truth

The source of truth for every email. **Never invent specs; if it isn't here, ask.**

## What it is

A **powdered yerba mate latte** — a coffee alternative, not a fruit-flavoured
powder. You pour it on water, mix, and it becomes a creamy latte. Hot or iced.

- **One SKU today: Mate Latte, Vanilla.** Just launched.
- **15 servings per bag.**
- **90 calories per scoop · 3g sugar.**

## The functional three (exact — do not change)

| Ingredient | Amount | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| Natural caffeine (from yerba mate) | **100mg** | Clean, sustained energy — no jitters, no crash |
| Lion's Mane | **500mg** | Cognitive support — focus and mental clarity |
| L-Theanine | **200mg** | Balanced, calm sensation; smooths the energy out |

## Full ingredient list

Oat milk powder, coconut milk powder, coconut cream powder, honey powder,
coconut sugar, acacia fiber, yerba mate extract, MCT oil powder (from coconut),
vanilla flavour, Lion's Mane mushroom extract, monk fruit extract, L-Theanine,
pink Himalayan salt.

- Sweetened with **honey and real monk fruit**.
- Made with **oat milk and coconut** (that's the creaminess).
- **Organic ingredients. No artificial sweeteners. Gluten-free and dairy-free.**
- **No cane sugar, no erythritol** — that's what the "no cane sugar / erythritol"
  icon claims. (It is a free-from claim, NOT the sweetener.)

## Customer

Health-conscious adults **25–42** with a daily hot-beverage ritual, actively
looking for something better than coffee. Professionals, remote workers,
entrepreneurs, wellness-forward. Likely aware of mushroom coffee, matcha lattes
and mushroom-matcha blends; may have heard about yerba mate through podcasts
(e.g. Huberman) but never found a convenient, good-tasting way to drink it —
it seemed too bitter or too complicated to prepare. The latte format is what
makes it accessible.

**Drinking moments:** the morning ritual, the 2pm afternoon latte, any latte
moment — hot or cold. Also pre-workout and for staying productive at home.

## The problem with the alternatives

| They drink | The problem |
|---|---|
| Coffee | Harsh crashes, jitters, anxiety, bad sleep |
| Matcha | Not enough caffeine to stay properly alert |
| Mushroom coffee | Very little caffeine, and it tastes bad |

**Milonga takes the best of all of them:** the right amount of clean caffeine,
Lion's Mane for mental clarity, L-Theanine to keep it balanced and calm — and
unlike the alternatives, it's genuinely delicious.

## Positioning

**One line:** everything your mornings are looking for — the right amount of
clean energy, mental clarity, and a calm, balanced start to the day.

**Competitors:** Yerba Magic, MUD\WTR, RISE, plus traditional coffee, mushroom
coffee brands and matcha/matcha-latte brands.

**Emotional promise:** an ancient herbal tea consumed for thousands of years —
a ritual, a culture, a way of being. Connection, slowing down, being in the
moment. Milonga brings that culture into an upgraded, delicious, ready-in-30-
seconds format you can keep at home in your own way.

**The name:** the founder is Argentinian; his grandparents danced *la milonga*.
The brand is a tribute to them.

## Voice

Playful, alive, inspiring, cultural.

## Claims — hard limits

**No medical or health claims.** Never say or imply that Milonga:
- cures anything
- makes you free of stress
- makes you lose weight
- makes you more intelligent

Stick to what's factual: caffeine content, ingredient amounts, "cognitive
support", "clean sustained energy", "balanced and calm", taste, convenience.
Yerba mate being antioxidant-rich and supporting metabolism is fine as general
ingredient context — never as a promise about the reader's body.

**Also never mention THC, CBD, cannabis or weed.**
