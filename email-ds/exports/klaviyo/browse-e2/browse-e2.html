Quick backstory: I’ve spent the last three years making yerba mate drinks with Milonga, and the whole time I kept wishing for one thing in the morning. One cup with everything my day needs: real energy that didn’t leave me wired, a clear head, and something I actually looked forward to drinking. So we made it.

Here’s what’s in every cup of the Mate Latte:

  • 100mg of natural caffeine from yerba mate, for coffee-level energy
  • 200mg of L-theanine, so that energy feels steady and calm, not jittery
  • 500mg of Lion’s Mane, the functional mushroom we add for focused, clear-headed mornings
  • Yerba mate itself, which brings antioxidants and vitamins, and supports a healthy gut, your immune system and your metabolism

All in a creamy vanilla latte that’s ready in 30 seconds, hot or iced.

People always ask me how it’s different from what they already drink, so here’s my honest take:

  • Coffee: great energy, but for a lot of us it comes with the jitters and a crash a few hours later.
  • Mushroom coffee: gentler, but it usually has much less caffeine (around 35–50mg), so it’s easy to barely notice, and it tastes earthy.
  • Matcha: calm and lovely, but subtle, and grassy if that’s not your thing.

The Mate Latte is the cup I wanted: coffee-level energy without the crash, the calm you get from matcha, the Lion’s Mane you’d find in mushroom coffee, and it tastes like a creamy vanilla latte.
