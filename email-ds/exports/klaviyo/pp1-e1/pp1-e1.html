We’re so glad you’re here. The Mate Latte is clean energy, focus and calm in a cup that tastes like a treat. Here’s everything you need to make it the right way, and the best moments to enjoy it.
How to make it
Three steps,
thirty seconds.
1
Scoop
2.5 tablespoons of Mate Latte into your mug or glass.
2
Pour
8oz of hot or cold water or milk. Milk makes it extra creamy.
3
Stir or froth
Stir it in, or froth it for a café-style foam.
Your way
Hot or iced,
it’s all yours.
Hot
Heat 8oz of milk or water, add 2.5 tablespoons and froth until smooth. Cozy and creamy.
Iced
Mix 2.5 tablespoons into 8oz of cold milk or water, then pour it over ice. Cold and refreshing.
When to enjoy it
Your first week
with Mate Latte.
 
First thing in the morning
Your new morning ritual
Make it your first cup of the day, in place of your coffee. Smooth, steady energy that comes in easy and a clear head to start the day. No jitters, no crash.
 
When it’s time to focus
Your deep-work partner
A long work session ahead, or that early-afternoon slump? It brings calm focus that carries you through. It has 100mg of caffeine, about a cup of coffee, so treat it like one later in the day.
 
With your people
