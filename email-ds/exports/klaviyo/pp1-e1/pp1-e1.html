How to make it
Three steps,
thirty seconds.
1
Scoop
2.5 tablespoons of Mate Latte into your mug or glass.
2
Pour
8oz of hot or cold water or milk. Milk makes it extra creamy.
3
Stir or froth
Stir it in, or froth it for a café-style foam.
Your way
Hot or iced,
it’s all yours.
Hot
Heat 8oz of milk or water, add 2.5 tablespoons and froth until smooth. Cozy and creamy.
Iced
Mix 2.5 tablespoons into 8oz of cold milk or water, then pour it over ice. Cold and refreshing.
