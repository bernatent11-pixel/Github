Make it at home
Two recipes,
one for each cup.
Hot
Mocha Mate Latte
 
•2.5 tbsp Mate Latte
•8oz milk
•1 tsp cocoa powder
Heat the milk, whisk in the cocoa and the Mate Latte, and froth. Top with a little shaved chocolate.
Iced
Iced Brown Sugar Oat Mate Latte
 
•2.5 tbsp Mate Latte
•8oz cold oat milk
•1 tsp brown sugar syrup
•Ice
Drizzle the syrup inside the glass, add ice, and pour in the Mate Latte mixed with the oat milk.
