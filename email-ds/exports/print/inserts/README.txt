PACKAGE INSERT CARDS · MATE LATTE · 4 x 6 in, printed both sides
================================================================

One card goes in every Mate Latte box. The FRONT is the same for every cohort; the BACK changes.

  Card A   first-time buyer        teach the first cup · ask for the review · introduce the club   DONE
  Card B   2+ orders, not in club  sell the club · ask for the review                               DONE
  Card C   first club order        how the club works, the portal, the perks                        next

Generated by: scripts/make-insert-assets.mjs (art) + scripts/make-insert-cards.mjs (layout, PDFs).
Edit the scripts, not the files here. Each card's copy is its block in CARDS (a, b) in
make-insert-cards.mjs; the prices card B prints are PRICE, at the top of that file.

PRINT SPEC (the same as the reference files)
  Trim 4 x 6 in, portrait. Bleed 0.125 in on every side, so each PDF page is 4.25 x 6.25 in
  (306 x 450 pt). No crop marks. Nothing that matters sits closer than 0.25 in to the trim.
  Colour is RGB (sRGB); let the printer convert to CMYK and ask for a printed proof of the gold
  (#E3BC62) and the dark green (#004D27) before the full run. Type is live vector with the fonts
  embedded; every image is 300 dpi or more at print size. The one exception to watch is the
  front's background: it was supplied 1200 px wide, which is 282 dpi across the card before it is
  resampled. Fine for satin, bubbles and a latte; if a larger original exists, drop it in as
  src-front-bg.jpg and rerun for crisper small print on the pouches.
  Stock: an uncoated or matte card, 14-16 pt. Gloss fights the pouch and makes QR codes glare.

WHERE THE QR CODES GO (checked live 2026-11-10: every one answers 301 and lands on a 200)

  Navigation > View URL redirects). That is ON PURPOSE: a redirect can be re-aimed any day, a
  printed code cannot. Each card has its own three paths, so scans can be told apart by cohort.

BEFORE ANYTHING IS PRINTED (the card promises these; none can be edited after the run)
  1 THE VIDEO IS A STAND-IN. There is no how-to video on the store: the product page has photos
    only, and there is no how-to page. /first-cup and /video-b point at the promo video because
    it is the one video that shows the latte being made (about 8 of its 32 seconds, with "Hot or
    iced" and "Ready in 30 seconds" on screen). It opens as a bare video file, with no page around
    it and no way on to the shop. When the real how-to exists, put its address in both redirects.
  2 The review reward has to be live. The card says "we'll email you 15% off your next order",
    the same promise the day-7 email makes. Set Judge.me to send a one-time 15% code after ANY
    review, good or bad (rewarding only good ones breaks FTC rules; the card says "honestly").
  3 Scan all three codes on a printed proof with an iPhone and an Android before approving the run.
    The generator already reads each code back from the 600 dpi render and refuses to finish if
    one does not decode, but paper, ink and finish are the printer's.
  4 CARD B PRINTS PRICES: $69.98 and $48.99 for 30 servings, $2.33 and $1.63 a cup. They are
    today's Shopify prices and the club's 30%. If either changes, card B is wrong on paper:
    change PRICE in the script and reprint. Print B in smaller runs than A for that reason.
  Card C needs its own three paths (for example /review-c), each with utm_campaign=card_c.

CARD A · WHAT IS ON IT AND WHY
  Front   Bernat's background (gold satin, two pouches, a latte), with the Milonga mark and
          "Thank you / Here's to better mornings." in the clear middle. The picture was wider than
          the card, so it was made taller rather than cropped: only an empty band of the gradient
          was stretched, and both pouches keep every letter. No offer and no cohort wording, so
          one print run of fronts serves all three cards.
  Back    1 Your first cup, done right: Scoop 2.5 tbsp · Pour 8oz hot or cold milk or water ·
            Stir or froth (the product page recipe, the same photos as the post-purchase emails).
            QR 1 (top right), "Watch the video".
          2 The gold panel, the loudest thing on the card because the review is this cohort's
            first job: "Leave a review, get 15% off." QR 2, "Write a review".
          3 The club, introduced and nothing more: save up to 30%, monthly, skip / pause / cancel.
            No discount code, no price, no deadline. The day-11 email does the selling.
            QR 3, "Join the club": the Mate Latte product page.
          4 "Something not right? Write to us first": an unhappy customer who emails is one who
            does not leave a one-star review.
          The small "A" bottom right is for whoever packs the box, so the right card goes in.
  Three QR codes is the ceiling for a card this size. They run top right, middle left, bottom
  right, each at least 2 in from the next, so a phone held to one frames one.
  All three are ONE style: the same 0.64 in code at the same density (29 x 29 modules), on a cream
  tile inside a forest outline, with a three-word label under it in the same type.

CARD B · WHAT IS ON IT AND WHY
  Same skeleton as card A, so the two read as a set: title with the video code top right, a
  feature row, the gold panel with a code on its left, a quiet closing block with a code on its
  right, the help line. What changes is which job gets the gold.
  Back    1 It opens on feeling, not on the offer: "You came back. / Thank you." and "A first
            order is curiosity. Coming back is trust, and to a small brand like ours, that means
            the world."
          2 The same two bags at two prices (30 servings is two 15-serving bags): ONE-TIME, full
            price $69.98, $2.33 a cup (outlined, grey) against IN THE CLUB, $69.98 struck through,
            $48.99, $1.63 a cup (filled gold, "Save 30%").
          3 "You might be the perfect fit for our subscription program. Join today and your very
            next order costs less." That second sentence is the card's urgency, and it is true:
            there is no deadline and no bag running out, only that the saving starts with the
            next order.
          4 The gold panel: "Subscribe for exclusive perks." + early access to new products ·
            exclusive store discounts · skip, pause or cancel anytime.
          5 "Got a minute? Leave a review." with NO 15% printed: a second discount on this card
            would compete with the one it is selling. Judge.me still sends the code to anyone who
            reviews.
  The only "30%" on the card is the badge on the 30-serving example it belongs to (15 servings
  is 15% off in the club), so nothing promises 30% on every size.
  No how-to steps: someone on their second bag knows how to make it. The video code stays, top
  right, because every card carries it.
  BEFORE CARD B PRINTS: "early access to new products" and "exclusive store discounts" are new
  promises (Bernat, 2026-11-10). Neither is on the product page or in the club emails today, and
  nothing in the store delivers them yet. They need a subscriber-only way to happen (a Klaviyo
  segment of active club members for early access, a members-only discount), and the club
  welcome email should say the same thing the card does.

FACTS USED (Shopify, checked 2026-11-10)
  Recipe: 2.5 tablespoons in 8oz of hot or cold water or milk, about 30 seconds.
  Mate Mornings Club (Appstle selling plans): 30% off 30 and 60 servings, 15% off 15 servings,
  monthly; skip, pause or cancel anytime. So the card says "up to 30%".
  NOT USED: "Save 10% next time" from the planning diagram. No 10% offer exists on the store, and
  it would be the weakest number on a card that already carries 15% (review) and 30% (club).
  Prices (card B): one-time $34.99 / $69.98 / $139.96 for 15 / 30 / 60 servings, $2.33 a cup at
  every size; in the club $48.99 / $97.97 for 30 / 60, $1.63 a cup.
  Help: orders@milongamate.com. No health or benefit claims on either card, so no FDA line.

HOW THE RIGHT CARD GETS IN THE RIGHT BOX
  The cohort has to be decided before the box is packed. Tag each order in Shopify Flow:
    order has a selling plan + customer's first subscription order  -> INSERT-C
    customer's order count is 1 (no selling plan)                   -> INSERT-A
    customer's order count is 2 or more (no selling plan)           -> INSERT-B
  and print the tag on the packing slip. Without this the three cards are one card.
