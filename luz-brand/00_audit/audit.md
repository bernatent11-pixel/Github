# Phase 0 — Audit: where LUZ stands today

**Sources read:** repo `index.html` (current LUZ-precursor landing page with inline-SVG tin and live palette tokens), `assets/README.md`, old Milonga THC/CBD packaging deck ("New Branding.pdf", Drive), Milonga content scripts and photo briefs (Drive), Milonga workspace structure (Notion). The investor-deck tin mockups referenced as `/inputs` were not found in this environment — see `decisions.md` #1. The landing page's tin is the same design language described for those mockups (cream tin, gold + forest green, rounded type), so the audit reads it as the current direction.

---

## 1. What the current LUZ direction gets right

- **The name and the metaphor.** LUZ (light) is short, ownable, pronounceable in English, and genuinely Argentine without being a costume. "Golden Clarity" locks the metaphor to the product truth (clean, clear energy) — the tagline and the name reinforce each other. This is the strongest asset in the system.
- **Cream as the base.** The warm cream (#f7efe0) field is the single best packaging decision made so far. It separates LUZ from every competitor at a glance (navy Ryze, black MUD\WTR, yellow Yerba Madre) and reads warm-kitchen rather than clinical-wellness.
- **Gold as a functional color, not decoration.** Gold is already being used to mean *light/clarity* — the right instinct. It maps to golden hour, to the color of the latte itself, and to the tagline.
- **Green kept as an accent, not the hero.** Mate heritage stays present without making LUZ "another green tea brand."
- **Format and tone instincts.** The tin (not a pouch) reads premium and ritual-object. The voice on the landing page is warm and human, not supplement-brochure.

## 2. What's weak

- **The display typeface (Fredoka) is the biggest liability.** Rounded, bubbly, geometric — it reads children's app / budget smoothie kiosk. At $34.99 it undercuts the price point directly (HARD NO: "too cheap"). It also has zero connection to mate culture or to light.
- **The gold is clip-art metallic.** The current 3-stop gold gradient (#ecc96f→#b98e38) simulates foil on screen and will disappoint in print. Gold needs to be either a real flat brand color with a separate printed-foil spec, or light rendered photographically — not a gradient pretending to be foil.
- **No symbol, no device, no pattern.** The system is a wordmark and two colors. Nothing to own at avatar size, nothing to repeat across a feed, no equity beyond the name. Social/TikTok channels will starve.
- **The tin layout is generic.** Centered stack: brand / product / servings. Swap the name and it could be any functional powder. Nothing says mate, ritual, or Argentina.
- **Mate culture is completely absent.** The positioning says ritual-first, ronda, presence — the current visuals say "beverage SKU." No gourd, no circle, no sharing, no Spanish, no golden hour. The gap between strategy and design is the whole brief.
- **Residual old-Milonga echoes.** Deep forest greens (#0e3a20 fields) and leaf-texture overlays sit close to the old THC-era look (HARD NO). The old can's "Uplifting Herbal Experience" illustrated-psychedelia must leave no trace: no trippy gradients, no herbal-leaf motifs, no "experience" language.
- **Two taglines in circulation.** "Energy That Thinks" is still live on the page; retired per decisions.md #4.

## 3. Equity to keep (carry into every direction)

1. LUZ name + light metaphor + "Golden Clarity"
2. Cream base field
3. Gold = light (recast from gradient to flat color + photographic light)
4. Green demoted to heritage accent (shifted away from old-Milonga forest/cannabis range)
5. Tin as a ritual object; warm, human voice

## 4. Competitor visual map (the 5 to avoid resembling)

| Brand | Palette | Type | Packaging codes | What they own |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ryze** | Royal/navy blue, white | Rounded friendly sans | Matte pouch, benefit bullets up front, UGC-driven | Clinical-cozy mushroom coffee; "doctor-approved morning swap" |
| **Four Sigmatic** | Black + yellow/orange, woodcut illustration | Quirky serif/sans mix | Illustrated boxes/pouches, joke-dense copy | Playful shroom-wellness whimsy |
| **MUD\WTR** | Monochrome earth: bone, clay, black | Brutalist stencil (the "\\") | Matte minimal canister, manifesto copy | Stoic anti-coffee masculinity |
| **Yerba Magic** | Green + white | Plain rounded sans | Amazon-native pouch, sparkle/leaf icons, benefit-led (confidence: training knowledge; thin public coverage) | Budget instant-mate powder |
| **Yerba Madre** (ex-Guayakí) | Iconic yellow can, red/green jungle | Organic serif/hand feel | Rainforest illustration, ROC gold sticker, activist copy ([Dieline](https://thedieline.com/yerba-madre-formerly-guayaki-utilized-its-die-hard-supporters-to-ensure-a-successful-rebrand/), [BevNET](https://www.bevnet.com/news/2025/one-mother-of-a-rebrand-guayaki-becomes-yerba-madre)) | Canned-mate heritage + regenerative mission |

**Pattern across all five:** every one leads with either *function* (Ryze, Magic), *quirk* (Four Sigmatic), *abstinence* (MUD\WTR) or *activism* (Yerba Madre). Nobody leads with *warmth, light, or the social ritual*. Palette-wise the field is blue / black / monochrome / yellow / green — cream + gold is unclaimed. Admired-brand cues to borrow structurally (not visually): Casalú's culture-first storytelling, Bloom's community surface, Liquid Death's cinematic self-belief.

## 5. The white space LUZ should own

LUZ should own **golden hour for the mind**: the warm, social, Argentine ritual of mate rendered as light — cream and gold where the category is dark and clinical, people-in-a-ronda where the category shows powder scoops and mushroom icons, a confident cultural voice (light Spanish sprinkle, cinematic warmth) where the category hedges with benefit bullets. The brand behaves like an invitation to slow down and share, not a prescription to optimize — premium in materials and photography, never in seriousness. No competitor can follow: Ryze/Magic are locked into clinical value, MUD\WTR into stoicism, Four Sigmatic into cartoon whimsy, Yerba Madre into activist jungle-yellow. Cream-and-gold warmth with real mate culture is open, defensible, and true to the product in the tin.
