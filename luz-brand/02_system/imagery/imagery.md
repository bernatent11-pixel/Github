# LUZ Imagery Direction

Two modes, carried from the Milonga foundation, now lit by LUZ:

- **Polished** (site, email, Amazon, brand posts): editorial, styled, Casalú-level. Golden-hour light is the brand's signature — warm, low, directional; long soft shadows; steam made visible by backlight. Grounds in beige/travertine/warm wood; forest + gold in props.
- **Raw** (TikTok, creators, performance ads): phone-shot, spontaneous, Bloom-style. Real tables, real friends, mid-conversation. The tin appears naturally, never "presented".

**Always:** warm whites (never clinical blue), people mid-moment (never posing at camera), the ritual visible (pouring, passing, whisking, sobremesa). **Never:** grassy-green wellness scenes, lab/clinical props, mushroom imagery, jungle-dense foliage (Yerba Madre's lane), anything that echoes cannabis culture, sad-desk-lunch energy.

**AI-image rule:** no logos or label text in generation prompts. Product shots use the approved tin as a reference image; **review every output for label distortion — if LUZ or any pack text warps, discard or composite the vector artwork instead.** People/lifestyle → `soul_2`. Product stills → `nano_banana_pro`. Motion → `seedance_2_0` (default) or `kling3_0` (single-character performance).
