# LUZ Voice Snapshot

The Milonga foundation voice, carried whole: **the Argentine friend everyone wants at the table.** Short sentences. "You" more than "we". Funny, never mean. Functional, never medicinal. The feeling leads; the facts back it up.

LUZ adds one register on top: **light**. Mornings, golden hour, clarity. When in doubt, write about the moment the light hits the table, not the ingredient that makes it possible.

## Five do / don't pairs

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| "Morning, aligned. Pour the light." | "Optimize your morning routine with nootropics." *(too wellness)* |
| "Che, your 2pm self called. It says thanks." | "Say goodbye to the afternoon crash forever!" *(promised result)* |
| "Made for the long sobremesa — the talk after the table's cleared." | "Engineered for maximum productivity." *(too serious, too supplement)* |
| "Supports focus you can actually feel." | "Boosts brainpower." *("boost" is banned; brain claims are a regulatory flag)* |
| "One tin. Fifteen slow mornings." | "CHEAPER THAN YOUR LATTE HABIT!!" *(too cheap; we compete on quality)* |

**Banned words (from the foundation):** boost · supercharge · detox · miracle · game-changing · "the only" · "the first" · any competitor's name. Claims always "supports", never "improves". No health promises anywhere in brand copy.

## Spanish sprinkle — glossary of 10

Rule of rules: **at most one Spanish word per piece** (post, subject line, panel). It seasons, never performs. An Argentine brand, never an American one doing mate. Always lowercase except sentence start; never italicized (it isn't foreign to us); never translated in-line except in education content.

| Word | Means | Use it for | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| mate | the drink & the ritual | everywhere | The category word; never pluralize as "matés". |
| ronda | the sharing circle | community moments, UGC prompts | Content only — never a product/program name (reserved per foundation). |
| cebar | to prepare/pour mate | how-to, ritual content | Pair with a visual; don't explain in ads. |
| che | hey / friend | social hooks, subject lines | Max 1 per piece; never in legal/packaging. |
| dale | let's go / okay | CTAs, replies | CTA flavor ("Dale — try it iced"), never on pack. |
| sobremesa | the lingering after a meal | brand storytelling, email | The most LUZ word we own; use monthly, not daily. |
| merienda | the afternoon snack ritual | 2–5pm content, recipes | Anchor for the afternoon-reset occasion. |
| tranquilo | easy, calm | reassurance copy | Adjective of the calm benefit; never "chill pill" jokes. |
| buenísimo | really good | reviews, reactions | Reaction word in community replies only. |
| asado | the cookout | lifestyle/event content | Scene word; LUZ is present at the asado, not the point of it. |

## Tagline

**Golden Clarity** — the only tagline. On the lid, in the tagline lockup, as a sign-off. Never translated, never punctuated, never "golden clarity in every cup"-style extensions. ("Energy That Thinks" and all Milonga-era lines are retired.)
