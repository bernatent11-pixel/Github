# LUZ Packaging — Regulatory Flags (open questions for counsel / regulatory consultant)

Nothing below is legal advice; every flagged item blocks final artwork. All panels in `tin-wrap-flat.svg` are placeholders.

1. **Supplement vs. conventional food classification.** The formula (yerba mate extract, 500mg Lion's Mane, 200mg L-theanine, oat/coconut creamer, honey + monk fruit) straddles the line. Lion's Mane at a functional dose and added L-theanine generally push toward **dietary supplement** labeling (Supplement Facts panel, FDA disclaimer box); a "latte mix" positioning suggests conventional food (Nutrition Facts). The choice changes the whole back panel. → Decide with counsel before dieline.
2. **Front-of-pack ingredient claims.** "500 MG LION'S MANE · 200 MG L-THEANINE · 100 MG NATURAL CAFFEINE" (as on the approved mockup) is quantitative ingredient labeling — generally permissible if truthful, but placement/format rules differ by classification above. Confirm.
3. **Structure/function claims.** Brand copy uses only "supports …" phrasing (per foundation). If the pack carries any (e.g., "supports focus"), supplement route requires the DSHEA disclaimer ("This statement has not been evaluated by the FDA…"). Current pack artwork carries **no** claims — keep it that way until counsel signs off.
4. **Caffeine disclosure.** 100mg/serving: add advisory ("Not recommended for children, pregnant or nursing women, or caffeine-sensitive individuals") — common practice; confirm wording.
5. **Honey.** Contains honey → **not for infants under 1 year** advisory (botulism risk) — placeholder already on panel; confirm requirement/wording for this format.
6. **Allergens.** Coconut = FDA tree nut for labeling purposes → "Contains: Coconut." Oat milk: confirm gluten-free status of supply (GF claim only with certification). FALCPA statement format pending ingredient sign-off.
7. **Net weight.** 9 oz / 255 g is a placeholder (15 × 17g scoop assumption). Confirm fill weight; dual declaration format (oz + g) required.
8. **"Dairy-free."** Permissible if true; avoid "non-dairy" (different FDA meaning). Confirm against final formula.
9. **Lion's Mane novel-ingredient status.** Hericium erinaceus fruiting body is commonly sold; confirm supplier spec + any state-level requirements (e.g., prop 65 testing for heavy metals is practically expected in CA retail/Amazon).
10. **Country of origin / "Product of".** Mate extract origin (Argentina/Brazil?) — confirm if origin claims are made in marketing.
11. **Legal entity line.** "Distributed by Milonga Life Corp, Doral FL" kept per LUZ-standalone decision (#9) — confirm entity name and address current.
12. **Dieline.** Ø99 × H142 mm tin is an assumption. **The real dieline, print process (offset litho / dry-offset / shrink), and metallic spec (PMS 871 vs hot foil vs metallized substrate) must come from the tin printer** before any final artwork.
