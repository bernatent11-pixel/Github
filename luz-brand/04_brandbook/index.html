Brand story

LUZ means light. It started with a ritual: in South America, mate has always been more than something you drink — it's a reason to slow down, share, and be present. Founded by father-and-son duo Samer and Shadi Atassi (Shadi learned mate from his teammates chasing a football career in Argentina), LUZ brings that ritual into modern life: real mate energy, in a creamy vanilla latte, ready in thirty seconds.

Position: the modern expression of mate — an ancient ritual of presence, balance, energy and connection, turned into delicious, functional products for everyday life. Mate culture first, benefits second. A lifestyle brand, never a supplement brand.

The product: Vanilla Mate Latte — organic yerba mate extract (100 mg natural caffeine), 500 mg Lion's Mane, 200 mg L-theanine. Creamy, dairy-free, honey + monk fruit, <3 g sugar, ~90 cal. Hot or iced. $34.99 · 15 servings.

06

Imagery

Two modes from the foundation: Polished (editorial, golden-hour, Casalú-level — site, email, Amazon) and Raw (phone-shot, Bloom-style — TikTok, creators, performance). Signature light: warm, low, directional; steam made visible by backlight. People mid-moment, never posing. Never: clinical props, mushroom imagery, grassy wellness, jungle density, anything cannabis-adjacent.

AI imagery rules: no logos or label text in prompts; the approved tin travels as a reference image; every output is reviewed for label distortion — warped text means discard or composite the vector artwork. People → soul_2 · product → nano_banana_pro · motion → seedance_2_0 / kling3_0. The full 17-prompt tested bank lives in 02_system/imagery/imagery.md.

07

Voice

The Argentine friend everyone wants at the table. Short sentences. "You" more than "we". Funny, never mean. Functional, never medicinal. The feeling leads, facts back it up — "supports", never "boosts". LUZ adds the register of light: write the moment the light hits the table, not the ingredient list.

✅ Do✕ Don't
"Morning, aligned. Pour the light.""Optimize your morning routine with nootropics."
"Che, your 2pm self called. It says thanks.""Say goodbye to the afternoon crash forever!"
"Made for the long sobremesa.""Engineered for maximum productivity."
"Supports focus you can actually feel.""Boosts brainpower."
"One tin. Fifteen slow mornings.""CHEAPER THAN YOUR LATTE HABIT!!"
